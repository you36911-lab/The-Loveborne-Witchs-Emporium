# Week two (Days 8–14): draft

A draft for the designer to read and change. Nothing here is in the game yet; once it is agreed, it goes into
`js/data/story.js` and `LAST_PLAYABLE_DAY` becomes 14.

Where it comes from:
- **Regulars**: the design document's Visit Schedule (Dottie Day 9, Edmund Day 10, Rosalind Day 10, Noor Day 12, Hester Day 12).
- **Walk-in orders**: the tracker's Walk-in Plan, Days 8–14 (13 recipes), unchanged except one move: The Solitude
  Sweetspot goes from Day 12 Sunrise (shared with Words Like Honey) to Day 12 Twilight, the hour that was free. Both can
  be made at any hour that day.
- **Advice**: the five hours left with no customer and no letter. As in week one, each question is answered by an entry
  whose keyword the book already shows (or a letter has just given), and teaches a thing an order needs a day or two later.
- **Hints**: every hint names one ingredient and a keyword its entry hides, needed soon (this week or early week three).
- **Auras**: the five colours week one did not use come in: dark knot, sparkling white, yellow, red and murky green.

What is no longer written: report lines and "oops" lines for orders. A spell is only given when it is right now, so a
walk-in never comes back to say how it went wrong. Advice keeps its three reactions (a Good answer still reports back).

Every new walk-in needs a picture (the regulars all have one).
